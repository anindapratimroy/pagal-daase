Research Areas & Specializations — DAASE, IIT Indore

Compact Objects & Transients

Studies of black holes, neutron stars, pulsars, fast radio bursts (FRBs), gamma-ray bursts (GRBs), and gravitational wave astrophysics.

Contributing Faculty: Dr. Manoneeta Chakraborty, Dr. Bhargav Vaidya, Dr. Amit Shukla, Dr. Deepika Bollimpalli, Dr. Mukul Bhattacharya

Cosmology

Probing the early universe, 21-cm cosmology, the Epoch of Reionization, Cosmic Dawn, Line Intensity Mapping, and large-scale structure formation.

Contributing Faculty: Prof. Abhirup Datta, Dr. Suman Majumdar, Dr. Priyanka Singh, Dr. Prakash Gaikwad

Galaxies & Active Galactic nuclei

Interstellar medium, dynamical modeling of galaxies, dark matter distribution in galaxies, circumgalactic medium, and computational astrophysics.

Contributing Faculty: Dr. Narendra Nath Patra, Dr. Priyanka Singh, Dr. Soumavo Ghosh, Dr. Amit Shukla, Dr. Mukul Bhattacharya

Solar Physics and Space Weather

Solar physics, ionospheric physics, and lower-upper atmosphere coupling.

Contributing Faculty: Dr. Bhargav Vaidya, Dr. Saurabh Das, Dr. Amit Shukla, Prof. Abhirup Datta

Remote Sensing & Atmospheric Science

Cloud and precipitation physics, polar meteorology, numerical weather prediction, radar meteorology, SAR, and remote sensing in Earth observation.

Contributing Faculty: Dr. Saurabh Das, Dr. Unmesh Khati

Instrumentation & Space Technology

Satellite-based navigation (GNSS/GPS/NavIC), satellite communication channels, IoT, small satellite systems, and radiation detector simulation.

Contributing Faculty: Prof. Abhirup Datta, Dr. Saurabh Das, Dr. Amit Shukla

Data Science

Big data, Bayesian machine learning, deep learning, quantum machine learning, and data visualization.

Contributing Faculty: Dr. Saurabh Das, Prof. Abhirup Datta, Dr. Bhargav Vaidya, Dr. Narendra Nath Patra, Dr. Manoneeta Chakraborty, Dr. Soumavo Ghosh, Dr. Prakash Gaikwad, Dr. Suman Majumdar, Dr. Deepika Bollimpalli, Dr. Priyanka Singh, Dr. Unmesh Govind Khati, Dr. Amit Shukla, Dr. Mukul Bhattacharya
