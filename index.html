About DAASE

The Department of Astronomy, Astrophysics and Space Engineering (DAASE) at IIT Indore is the only dedicated department of its kind among all IITs, offering a comprehensive academic ecosystem spanning the cosmos to cutting-edge space engineering.

A founding member of the Square Kilometre Array – India Consortium (SKA-IC) since 2015, DAASE plays a significant role in global astronomical collaboration, with faculty contributing to major ISRO and NASA missions.

Degree Programs

The only IIT department with M.Sc. Astronomy and the first IIT with B.Tech. in Space Science & Engineering.

Undergraduate · 4 Years

B.Tech. in Space Science & Engineering

A pioneering program — the first B.Tech. of its kind in the IIT system (2022). Integrates rigorous space science fundamentals with engineering applications, preparing graduates for careers in Indian and global space sectors.

Space science fundamentals: orbital mechanics, rocket propulsion, remote sensing
Engineering applications: instrumentation, satellite systems, RF systems
Data science, computational methods, and hands-on lab training
Minor in Astronomy & Space Science available for IIT Indore students
2022
First B.Tech. in Space Science & Engineering across all IITs
3
Active batches (2023, 2024, 2025) currently enrolled
6+

Postgraduate · 2 Years

M.Sc. in Astronomy

Launched May 2018 — making DAASE the first and only IIT department to offer M.Sc. Astronomy. Rigorous training from classical to modern observational and theoretical astronomy.

Classical and modern observational astronomy, stellar astrophysics
Radio astronomy, optical observations, and instrumentation
Cosmology, galactic dynamics, high-energy astrophysics
Research project with faculty supervision in final year
2018
First M.Sc. Astronomy program across all IITs

12+

Postgraduate · 2 Years

M.Tech. in Space Engineering

Launched in 2021, bridging advanced space technology with practical engineering — RF systems, remote sensing, satellite instrumentation, and space mission design with research exposure.

RF engineering: system design, antenna theory, up to 60 GHz testing
Satellite systems, CubeSat design, and space mission architecture
Remote sensing, SAR, hyperspectral imaging, atmospheric sounding
Thesis-based with strong industry & ISRO linkages
2021
Two specializations: Space Engineering and AOLT

M.S. (Research) in Space Science & Engineering

Launched in 2021, designed for students who wish to pursue intensive research before a Ph.D. Students work closely with faculty on cutting-edge projects in space science and engineering.
